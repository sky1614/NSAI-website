FAQ

Common
questions.

Do you work with clients outside India? ▾
Yes. We work with businesses globally — US, UK, Canada, Australia, and Southeast Asia. We operate async-first with timezone overlap for synchronous calls.
How long does a typical project take? ▾
A simple website: 1–2 weeks. A custom AI agent: 3–6 weeks. An intelligent dashboard: 4–8 weeks depending on data complexity.
Do you provide ongoing support after delivery? ▾
Yes. Post-launch support is included in every engagement. We do not hand over and vanish.
What is your pricing? ▾
Every project is scoped individually. Indicative ranges are on the Services page. The discovery call is always free.
Can you work within a tight budget? ▾
We are honest about what is possible at different price points. If your budget does not fit what you need, we will tell you rather than overpromise and underdeliver.
Do you sign NDAs? ▾
Yes. We are comfortable signing NDAs before detailed project discussions.
