Executive Summary

LeadGen AI
Autonomous B2B Outreach.

Agentic AI platform that automates the entire B2B outreach pipeline — from finding leads to booking meetings — with zero manual work.

Live in Production SaaS · B2B India-first
The Problem

3–4 hours lost daily
to manual prospecting.

Problem
Manual, generic, mistimed outreach

Indian B2B sales teams waste 3–4 hours daily searching directories, copying contacts, writing cold emails one by one. Conversion rates stay below 2% because outreach is generic and sent to the wrong people at the wrong time.

Gap in existing tools
Apollo & Salesforce ignore India

Existing tools like Apollo and Salesforce are built for US markets — expensive, complex, and ignore India-specific channels like WhatsApp, IndiaMART, and regional languages. No tool covers the full pipeline end to end.

Technology

11 AI techniques
in production.

Self-Improvement
  • OPRO — self-optimizing prompts that improve with each campaign
  • Reflexion — learns from failures, applies lessons to future runs
  • A/B Testing — statistically significant prompt optimization
  • Autonomous Loop — self-corrects when reply rates drop
Intelligence
  • GNN — graph neural network scores leads on industry relationships
  • MemGPT — three-tier memory across campaigns
  • ReAct — reasoning traces for every outreach decision
  • RAG — retrieves best-performing messages as few-shot examples
  • LLM-as-Judge — evaluates every message on 5 dimensions before sending
  • Constitutional AI — 10 rules ensuring ethical, compliant outreach
  • Reply Intent Detection — classifies replies as positive/negative/neutral
