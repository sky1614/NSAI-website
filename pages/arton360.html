Executive Summary

ArtOn360
Multi-Vendor Print-on-Demand Marketplace.

Empowering independent artists to design, publish, and monetize custom apparel — without any technical, manufacturing, or logistics knowledge.

Live E-Commerce · POD Multi-Vendor
The Problem

Artists have talent.
Not infrastructure.

Problem
Limited control on existing platforms

Independent artists on Redbubble or Merch by Amazon have limited control over pricing, branding, storefront design, and customer relationships. Monetization infrastructure is scarce — especially in emerging markets like India.

Solution
Full vendor ownership

ArtOn360 gives artists their own storefront, their own pricing, their own album collections, and direct earnings notifications every time a sale happens — powered by a browser-based design tool that requires zero technical knowledge.

Key Features

Built for artists.
Powered by automation.

🎨
AI-Assisted Design Studio
Browser-based tool where artists upload artwork, preview on 33+ T-shirt color variants in real time, and publish to the marketplace in under 5 minutes — no design software needed.
⚡
60-Second Publish Pipeline
From design tool to live product page in under 60 seconds — auto-generates 297 color/size variations, sets pricing, assigns artwork overlay, and sends the artist a confirmation email automatically.
🏷️
Automated Launch Discounting
Every new design automatically receives a 10% launch discount for 48 hours — giving artists immediate visibility and sales momentum with zero manual intervention.
🏪
Multi-Vendor Revenue Infrastructure
Each artist gets a dedicated storefront, album organization system, and automated earnings notifications — powered by Dokan + WooCommerce handling payouts, order tracking, and vendor analytics independently per seller.
🔍
Global Product Search
Color synonym mapping and artist name lookup so customers find exactly what they want — even if they search "navy" instead of "dark blue".
📧
Automated Email Pipeline
Publish confirmation, sale notification, and customer thank-you emails — all triggered automatically. No manual follow-up needed from the artist.
🛡️
Content Filtering & Role-Based Navigation
Mature content filtering with blur overlay system. Role-based navigation separates customer and vendor experiences cleanly.
