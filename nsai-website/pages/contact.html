FAQ

Common questions

How long does it take to deploy an AI agent? ▾
Most agents go live within 24 hours of onboarding. Complex custom integrations (Tally, custom CRM) may take 48–72 hours.
Do I need technical knowledge to use your agents? ▾
Zero technical knowledge required. We handle all setup, integration and configuration. You just use the dashboard and WhatsApp like normal.
Can the agents speak Hindi and other Indian languages? ▾
Yes — our agents support Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and English. We can configure the primary language based on your customers.
What's the minimum commitment? ▾
Month-to-month. No annual lock-in for the Starter plan. Growth plan clients get better rates on 3-month or 6-month commitments.
Can you build a completely custom agent for our business? ▾
Yes — that's one of our specialties. We've built custom agents for BFSI, manufacturing, EdTech and retail. Book a call to discuss your requirements.
Is our data secure? ▾
All data is processed and stored in compliance with India's DPDP Act. We never share your data with third parties. Enterprise clients get on-premise deployment options.
